# Done

Sprints that are finished: built, reviewed, landed, and through their `/retro`. The retro moves
a sprint's whole folder here — its spec, its stories, its `review.md` and its `retro.md` — and
its row then leaves the board. `S-000-maintenance/` in here collects the standing sprint's
finished stories, moved as each is recorded.

Nothing here is edited again. What the product does today is `docs/FEATURES.md`; a folder here
says how one part of it came to be.
