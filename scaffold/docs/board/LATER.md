# Later

> What is not being built. A planning session reads this before it proposes anything, and adds
> to it when the owner parks an idea or says no (`docs/PLANNING.md`). Add or replace an entry;
> never reorder the file. Nothing here is scheduled, and nothing here justifies an abstraction
> today (directive 10).

## Parked

Wanted, but not now. A line each: what, and who asked when. An idea leaves this list when a
sprint is planned for it.

## Decided against

Asked for, and refused. The reason must still be true in a year — "no time" is a parking, not a
refusal. When the idea comes back, say what was decided and ask whether that still holds.

- **_[idea]_** — _[why not, in one or two sentences; the date]_
